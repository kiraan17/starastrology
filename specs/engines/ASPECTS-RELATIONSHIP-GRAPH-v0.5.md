# Chart Aspects & Relationship Graph (P08b v0.5)

**Module:** `bhava360.chart.aspects`  
**Techniques:** TEC-018, TEC-019, TEC-020 (dispositor graph completed)

## In scope

- Whole-sign **graha aspects** with special aspects (Mars 4/8, Jupiter 5/9, Saturn 3/10)
- Provisional Rahu/Ketu 5/7/9 special aspects (labelled)
- **Jaimini rashi drishti** (movable/fixed/dual rules) — never labelled Gemini
- Sign conjunctions
- Dispositor edges + dispositor chains
- Unified relationship graph nodes/edges
- Included by default in `ChartConstructor` (`bhava360-kernel-0.5.0`)

## Out of scope

- Degree-based aspect orbs / partial strength
- Argala (separate technique)
- Aspect-based yoga expansion beyond existing Parashara thin slice
